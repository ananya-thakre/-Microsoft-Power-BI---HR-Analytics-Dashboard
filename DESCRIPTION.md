This HR Analytics Dashboard is a comprehensive Power BI solution designed to transform human resources data into actionable business intelligence. It provides HR professionals and organizational leaders with powerful tools to track employee metrics, analyze workforce trends, monitor attrition patterns, and evaluate departmental performance through interactive visualizations and drill-down capabilities. The dashboard consolidates critical HR data including headcount information, recruitment metrics, employee demographics, performance KPIs, and historical trends into a unified platform that enables data-driven decision-making for talent management, retention strategies, and organizational planning.
